What information PetLife handles, why it is used, and the controls available to you.PetLife 处理哪些资料、为何使用，以及你可以使用哪些控制方式。PetLife が取り扱う情報、その利用目的、お客様が利用できる管理方法について説明します。PetLife가 처리하는 정보와 이용 목적, 사용자가 선택할 수 있는 관리 방법을 설명합니다.

When you sign in, enter, import, capture, upload, or purchase, PetLife processes the information described below to provide its care-record and family-coordination features.

Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, this may include account and display identifiers; an installation device identifier; pet and household profiles and contact numbers; daily, health, and medical records; photos, videos, audio, and documents you add; AI prompts and generated results; and subscription entitlements.

We use this information for authentication, records, reminders, household collaboration, cloud sync, AI organization when requested, export, purchase verification, and security.

Household sharing

People in the same household can view or edit information only within the role granted by the owner. Invitations, temporary care, and medical-data actions are limited by permissions, expiry, and owner confirmation. Invite only people you trust and have informed.

Cloud sync and attachments

After sign-in, structured records sync through PetLife servers. Backup of attachments to private cloud storage depends on your entitlement and selection. If sync conflicts or authentication expires, sync pauses and PetLife shows a notice. Photos, originals, and exported files are not made public automatically.

Service providers and AI

Apple handles sign-in and App Store subscriptions. Alibaba Cloud hosts PetLife APIs, databases, private object storage, and some on-demand image capabilities. Only when you start an AI feature are the prompt and task-required context sent through the PetLife gateway to DeepSeek. Image tasks may also use Alibaba Cloud AI services.

PetLife does not sell data to advertisers or use it for cross-app tracking.

Health information

Health-related information is used for care reminders, fact organization, and communication with your veterinarian. PetLife does not diagnose, prescribe, or calculate dosage.

Retention and security

Information stored on your device remains until you delete a record, reset data, or delete your account. Cloud information is kept while needed to provide accounts, household collaboration, subscriptions, security auditing, or to meet legal obligations.

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Attachments use private storage and signed access, and household actions are checked against roles. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices, export, and deletion

In PetLife’s Account & Data settings, you can export a local summary, delete cloud attachments, or permanently delete your account. You can stop using AI or turn off attachment backup to stop those optional processes.

Account deletion completes after the server confirms Apple credential revocation and cleanup of account-exclusive cloud data. Household audit records that must remain are de-identified.

AI は資料の整理、要約の作成、Life Book の文章案、獣医師との連絡資料の準備に利用できます。AI タスクを開始すると、必要な入力と文脈が PetLife のゲートウェイ経由で DeepSeek に送信されます。画像タスクでは Alibaba Cloud の AI サービスを利用する場合があります。AI の結果が誤っている可能性があるため、利用前に出典と事実を確認してください。

医療に関する制限

PetLife はケア記録とコミュニケーションを支援するツールです。診断、処方、投薬量の計算は行いません。緊急、異常、または判断に迷う場合は、獣医師または地域の救急窓口にご相談ください。

サービスの提供状況

クラウド同期、SMS、Apple サインイン、サブスクリプション、AI 機能は各サービス提供者の提供状況に依存します。サービスが利用できない場合、PetLife はその状態を表示し、端末内の情報を通知なく破棄しません。

멤버십 및 구독

멤버십은 자동 갱신 구독입니다. 가격, 결제 주기 및 체험 제공 여부는 구매 화면에 표시된 App Store 정보를 따릅니다. 구매 확인 후 Apple 계정으로 결제되며, 현재 기간이 끝나기 최소 24시간 전에 취소하지 않으면 자동 갱신됩니다.

Apple 구독 설정에서 구독을 관리하거나 취소할 수 있습니다. 구입 복원, 환불 및 이용 권한 확인은 App Store와 서버의 결과를 따릅니다.

AI 기능

AI는 자료 정리, 요약 생성, Life Book 문안 초안 작성 및 수의사와의 상담 자료 준비에 사용할 수 있습니다. AI 작업을 시작하면 필요한 입력과 맥락이 PetLife 게이트웨이를 거쳐 DeepSeek로 전송됩니다. 이미지 작업에는 Alibaba Cloud AI 서비스가 사용될 수 있습니다. AI 결과는 틀릴 수 있으므로 사용하기 전에 출처와 사실을 확인해 주세요.
