What PetLife does

PetLife is a care record and vet-visit communication aid.

Does PetLife diagnose or prescribe?

No. It organizes saved facts, reminders, photos, and visit notes. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or calculate dosage.

What should I bring to a vet visit?

Bring the recent timeline, current observations, medications, and any questions you want to confirm with your veterinarian.

Can I use this site as the App Store listing link?

No. The App Store listing link will be available after the app is released. In App Store Connect, use the public marketing-site address for Marketing URL and the public support-page address for Support URL.
