When the visit matters

Bring the facts.
Keep the conversation clear.

PetLife helps you prepare a visit summary from saved records: recent changes, key abnormalities, medications, medical history, and the questions you want to ask.

Clear boundaries. PetLife organizes facts and communication. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or calculate dosage.
